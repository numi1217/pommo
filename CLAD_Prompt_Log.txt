The initial build of Desk Companion was created and iterated on in an earlier AI-assisted session
in Lens Studio 5.23. Work in that session, as reconstructed from a summary, covered:

  the Lens Studio `quat.lookAt` API.
- General iteration on distraction-detection tuning, book/prop visibility tied to the focus
  session lifecycle, and material/tint exclusions for specific creature sub-objects (e.g. a face
  mesh and an embedded book prop).
- Initial planning for a Lens Studio 5.23 -> 5.15.4 downgrade (for Spectacles 2024 device
  compatibility), captured as a standing project note but not yet executed.
